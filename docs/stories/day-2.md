# Day 2 — The Arctic Animals

## 英文小故事

**The Arctic Animals**

The Arctic is very cold.  
A caribou walks across the tundra.  
A large moose stands near a conifer.  
The moose has two big antlers.  
A bear cub follows its mother.  
A wolf pup plays in the snow.  
The wolves look for food together.  
Few tall trees grow on the tundra.  
The conifer has green needles.  
These animals can live in cold places.

## 中文意思

北极非常寒冷。  
一只北美驯鹿走过苔原。  
一只大驼鹿站在一棵针叶树旁。  
驼鹿有两只大鹿角。  
一只小熊跟着熊妈妈。  
一只小狼崽在雪地里玩。  
狼群一起寻找食物。  
苔原上很少生长高大的树木。  
针叶树长着绿色的针叶。  
这些动物能生活在寒冷的地方。

## 听力测试题

1. Where does the caribou walk?  
A. Across the tundra  
B. Across the desert  
C. Across a sports field

2. What stands near the conifer?  
A. A bear cub  
B. A large moose  
C. An astronaut

3. What does the moose have?  
A. Broad wings  
B. Sharp teeth  
C. Big antlers

4. What do the wolves look for?  
A. A ship  
B. Food  
C. A nest

**答案：** 1-A，2-B，3-C，4-B
