# Day 1 — A Nest in the Tree

## 英文小故事

**A Nest in the Tree**

I walk in the park with my dad.  
We discover a small nest in a tree.  
An adult bird carries a twig to the nest.  
A little pup sits under the tree.  
The tree is about three meters tall.  
Its branches have a broad shape.  
I see yellow pollen on a flower.  
Dad says, “Please do not touch the nest.”  
We watch the bird quietly.  
It is an exciting discovery.

## 中文意思

我和爸爸在公园里散步。  
我们在一棵树上发现了一个小鸟巢。  
一只成年鸟叼着一根小树枝飞向鸟巢。  
一只小狗崽坐在树下。  
这棵树大约三米高。  
它的树枝形成了宽大的形状。  
我看到花上有黄色的花粉。  
爸爸说：“请不要碰鸟巢。”  
我们安静地观察小鸟。  
这是一次令人兴奋的发现。

## 听力测试题

1. What do the child and Dad discover?  
A. A hollow tree  
B. A small nest  
C. A ship

2. What does the adult bird carry?  
A. A twig  
B. A bean  
C. A bookmark

3. Where does the pup sit?  
A. In the nest  
B. On a branch  
C. Under the tree

4. What is on the flower?  
A. Syrup  
B. Pollen  
C. Grain

**答案：** 1-B，2-A，3-C，4-B
