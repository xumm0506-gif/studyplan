# Day 3 — A Trip into Space

## 英文小故事

**A Trip into Space**

I read a book about our planet.  
The author writes about the Earth and space.  
Earth is the planet where we live.  
People and animals need oxygen.  
We breathe out carbon dioxide.  
An astronaut travels into space in a ship.  
The ship is filled with gas.  
The astronaut sees a million stars.  
I imagine flying above the Earth.  
I want to discover a new planet one day.

## 中文意思

我读了一本关于我们星球的书。  
作者写了地球和太空。  
地球是我们生活的行星。  
人和动物都需要氧气。  
我们呼出二氧化碳。  
宇航员乘飞船进入太空。  
飞船里充满了气体。  
宇航员看到无数颗星星。  
我想象自己飞在地球上方。  
我希望有一天能发现一颗新行星。

## 听力测试题

1. What is the book about?

C. A sports field

2. What do people and animals need?

B. Syrup  
C. Steel

3. What do we breathe out?  
A. Gas only  
B. Pollen  
C. Carbon dioxide

4. What does the child imagine?  
A. Cooking in an office  
B. Flying above the Earth  
C. Walking in a desert
